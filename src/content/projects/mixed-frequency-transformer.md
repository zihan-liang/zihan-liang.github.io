title: Mixed-Frequency Transformer for USD/CNH Volatility Forecasting
period: "2025"
role: Model developer and third author
category: time-series
summary: A multi-stream Transformer that aligns daily technical signals with lower-frequency macroeconomic and geopolitical information for multi-horizon volatility forecasting.
contributions:
  - Implemented temporal and positional encodings.
  - Proposed and implemented cross-attention using daily technical representations as queries and macroeconomic and geopolitical streams as keys and values.
  - Produced pooled and horizon-specific evaluation tables, predicted-versus-actual plots, and workflow figures.
  - Developed Integrated Gradients heatmaps for analysis around the 2024 U.S. presidential election period.
methods:
  - Mixed-frequency learning
  - Cross-attention
  - Multi-horizon forecasting
  - Integrated Gradients
results:
  - Evaluated 1-, 5-, and 10-day forecast horizons.
  - Contributed to an SSRN preprint presented at IBEC 2026 by a co-author.
